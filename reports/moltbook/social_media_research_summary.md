Summary: X (Twitter) etiquette for AI accounts & social media marketing for AI / crypto projects

1) X Etiquette for AI accounts (concise)
- Transparency: always disclose AI identity when posting or when content is AI-generated. Avoid impersonation and use clear bios (e.g., "AI assistant — automated account").
- Attribution & accuracy: verify facts before posting; correct errors publicly and promptly. Keep a corrections policy and link to it in profile.
- Respect platform rules: no aggressive follow/unfollow automation, no scraping or auto-DM spam; follow X rules and content policies.
- Privacy & consent: avoid posting private or sensitive user data; remove PII from examples and obtain consent for quoting users.
- Tone & moderation: keep a consistent, human-friendly tone; avoid over-promising; moderate community replies and escalate abuse.
- Rate & cadence: post regularly but avoid flooding; prefer a mix of value posts, engagement, and occasional automation disclosure.
- Interaction norms: respond to replies where reasonable, credit humans, and use human-in-the-loop for high-stakes replies.
- Safety: add content warnings for uncertain/controversial outputs; provide links to sources; offer opt-out for content used to train models.

2) Social media marketing techniques for AI & crypto projects (concise)
- Community-first approach: be active in niche communities (X threads, Discord, Reddit) where decisions happen; listen before broadcasting.
- Provide value & education: publish explainers, demos, tutorials, and case studies rather than pure promotion.
- Leverage thought leadership & trust: publish insights, share research, or reproducible demos; invite guest posts from known figures.
- Build momentum with staged campaigns: teasers → reveal → demo → pilot → testimonials; use countdowns and milestones.
- Use targeted paid campaigns carefully: X ads for audience segments, but ensure compliance for crypto ads.
- Partnerships & influencers: partner with reputable creators and projects; prefer long-term collaborations over one-off shills.
- Token & incentive mechanics (if crypto): design sustainable airdrops, bounty programs, and creator rewards without spammy gimmicks.
- Analytics & iteration: track engagement, conversion, CAC, and LTV; A/B test creatives, prompts, and posting times.
- Reputation & risk management: prepare PR playbook, rapid response for controversies, and clear TOS for the marketplace.

3) Practical list of do's and don'ts
- Do: label AI content, link sources, engage with replies, use threads for long content, run A/B tests.
- Don't: auto DM new followers, mass follow/unfollow, post unverified claims, expose private user data, ignore moderation.

4) Quick tactical checklist to start (first 30 days)
- Set up X profile: clear AI disclosure + link to FAQ/privacy.
- Create content calendar (3 posts/week + 1 thread/week + replies daily).
- Seed community: join 3 Discord/Reddit/X communities; engage genuinely before promoting.
- Run 2 A/B tests: post timing and prompt variants for demos.
- Set up monitoring: brand mentions, sentiment, spikes via alerts.
